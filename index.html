About Us

Professional tactical security solutions built on discipline, intelligence, and rapid response

Alpha Point Tactical Security (Pty) Ltd is a professional private security services provider specializing in armed response, tactical security operations, K9 deployment, and integrated alarm monitoring solutions.

We serve townships, suburbs, gated estates, and commercial districts, offering scalable security solutions designed to meet real-world risks. Our operations are built on discipline, intelligence, rapid response, and visibility, ensuring our clients are protected at all times.

Our teams are carefully selected, trained, and equipped to operate under pressure, delivering measurable security outcomes rather than empty promises.
